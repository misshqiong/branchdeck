A few useful details.

Does it replace my coding tools?

No. BranchDeck organizes local projects, commands, and terminals around your tools. It does not run or coordinate AI conversations.

Will terminals survive an app restart?

Switching workspaces or hiding the window keeps sessions alive while BranchDeck is running. Fully quitting stops its terminals and managed services. Sessions are not restored after a restart.

What does it need?

An Apple Silicon Mac, zsh, and Git for repository features. Your project commands still need their usual runtimes and dependencies. This download is distributed directly, outside the Mac App Store.
